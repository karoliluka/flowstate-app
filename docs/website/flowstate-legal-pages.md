# Privacy Policy

**flowstate**
Last updated: 20 November 2026

flowstate is a focus app for iPhone made by Luka Karoli in Slovenia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app stores, where, and why. The short version: your focus sessions stay on your phone. If you join a study group, your group mates see your name and your focus totals, and nothing else.

## What stays on your phone

These never leave your iPhone, and I can't see them:

- **Focus sessions:** when you focused, for how long, and on which subject.
- **Subjects:** names and colours.
- **Stats:** totals, streaks, the calendar, milestones and personal bests.
- **Settings:** appearance, notification times, your display name.
- **Allowed and blocked apps.** flowstate uses Apple's Screen Time. Apple only gives the app anonymous tokens for the apps you choose, so flowstate never learns which apps they are.

**Notifications and the Lock Screen timer** are created on your phone. They aren't sent from a server.

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it.

## Study groups and accounts

Study groups are optional. Everything else in flowstate works without an account.

### What I store when you create an account
- **Login details:** your email address and a securely hashed password. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, I store an identifier from them instead of a password. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email; Apple then gives a private relay address.
- **Your name:** your first and last name. Apple or Google may provide them when you sign in with them.

### What your group mates see
- your **first and last name**,
- your **total focus time**, all time,
- your **focus time this week** (Monday to Sunday),
- **whether you're focusing right now**, and since when.

They don't see your email, your subjects, your individual sessions or anything about your apps.

### What else is stored for groups
The groups you belong to, their names, their invite codes, and when you joined.

### Why
To run the study groups you choose to use. Under the GDPR, the legal basis is performing the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)).

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Data transferred there is covered by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where applicable.

**I don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.**

## Invite links

When you share an invite link, anyone who opens it can see the group's name, the first name of the person who created the group, and how many members it has. Share links only with people you want in the group.

## How long data is kept

Your account data is kept until you delete your account.

**Deleting your account:** go to **Profile → Account → Delete account** in the app. This immediately removes:
- your login,
- your name and focus totals,
- your group memberships.

Groups you created stay for the other members. Backup copies kept by the hosting provider are removed on its regular backup schedule.

Signing out keeps your account but stops sharing updates.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:
- **see** the data I hold about you,
- **correct** it,
- **delete** it,
- **restrict** or **object to** its processing,
- **take a copy** of it (data portability).

Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, write to [contact email].

You can also complain to the Slovenian data protection authority: Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si.

## Children

flowstate is meant for students, but study groups require an account. If you're under 15, the age of digital consent in Slovenia, please ask a parent or guardian before creating one. Everything else in flowstate works without an account.

## Security

- **Encrypted connections:** all connections to the server use HTTPS.
- **Access rules:** the database only lets each person read their own data and that of people in their groups.
- **Passwords:** stored only as secure hashes, never in plain text.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, I'll also tell you in the app.

# Terms of Service

**flowstate**
Last updated: 20 November 2026

These terms apply when you use the flowstate app and its study groups. By using flowstate, you agree to them.

## 1. What flowstate is

flowstate helps you focus:
- it times focus sessions,
- it blocks distracting apps with Apple's Screen Time while a session runs,
- it shows your stats,
- it lets you focus alongside friends in study groups.

flowstate is free.

## 2. Blocking has limits

App blocking relies on Apple's Screen Time and works within its rules:
- **Phone and emergency calls** are never blocked.
- **Apps in Settings → Screen Time → Always Allowed** can't be blocked by any app.
- **iOS can change** how Screen Time works at any time.

flowstate is a self-control tool, not parental control or a security product. Don't rely on it where missing a call or a message could cause harm.

## 3. Your account

- **Your details:** give a real name your group mates will recognise, and keep your login details to yourself.
- **Your responsibility:** you're responsible for what happens under your account.
- **Deleting:** you can delete your account at any time in **Profile → Account → Delete account**.

## 4. Study groups

- **Who can join:** anyone with a group's invite link or code can join it. Only share it with people you want in the group.
- **Be decent:** don't use names or group names that are offensive, impersonate someone, or harass other people.
- **Accurate numbers:** group mates see the focus time you report. Don't try to fake or inflate it.
- **Removals:** I may remove groups, names or accounts that break these rules.

## 5. Your data

How your data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy at https://lukakaroli.si/flowstate/privacy. In short: your sessions stay on your phone, and groups only see your name and focus totals.
